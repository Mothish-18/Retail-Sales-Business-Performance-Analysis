# Retail Sales & Business Performance Analysis

## 1. Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large amounts of sales data every day. However, understanding sales performance, product profitability, customer purchasing patterns and regional performance can be challenging without proper data analysis.

This project analyzes retail sales data to identify business performance trends, understand profitable products and categories, evaluate customer contributions and compare regional sales performance.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can support better business decisions.

## 2. Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Analyze overall sales revenue and profit.
2. Identify the best-performing product categories.
3. Find the top 10 products based on revenue and profit.
4. Analyze monthly sales trends.
5. Identify the top 10 customers by revenue.
6. Compare sales performance across different regions.
7. Calculate important business KPIs.
8. Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 3. Key Business Questions

This project aims to answer the following questions:

- What is the total revenue and profit?
- Which category generates the highest revenue?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- Which region performs best in sales?
- How does revenue change over time?
- Who are the top 10 customers?
- What is the overall profit margin?
- Which categories sell the highest quantity?

- Dataset type: Synthetic retail sales dataset.

- Number of regions: 5.
